## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Which online PDF tool is completely free?

**A:** PDF Candy offers the most generous free tier with all 95+ tools available (ad-supported). iLovePDF and Sejda also have good free tiers with limitations. For developers, PDF.co offers free credits to test the API.

### Q: Are online PDF tools safe for sensitive documents?

**A:** Most reputable tools use encryption and auto-delete files. Sejda and PDF.co have the strongest privacy policies. For highly sensitive documents, consider desktop solutions or PDF.co's enterprise options with enhanced security.

### Q: Can I use these tools for commercial purposes?

**A:** Yes, all paid plans allow commercial use. Free tiers typically have restrictions on commercial usage. Check each tool's terms of service for specific details.

### Q: Which tool has the best API for automation?

**A:** PDF.co is the clear winner with 100+ API endpoints, comprehensive documentation, and integrations with Make, Zapier, and n8n.

### Q: Do these tools support batch processing?

**A:** PDF.co, iLovePDF, and SodaPDF (desktop) support batch processing. Others process files one at a time on free tiers.

### Q: Which tool provides the best conversion quality?

**A:** PDF.co and Smallpdf lead in conversion quality, with PDF.co offering more format options and AI-powered optimization.

### Q: Can I edit text in existing PDFs?

**A:** Yes, all tools offer basic PDF editing. Sejda's intelligent font matching and PDF.co's advanced editing provide the best results.

### Q: How do I choose the right PDF tool?

**A:** Consider your needs:
- **Automation/API**: PDF.co
- **Casual use**: iLovePDF or Smallpdf
- **Privacy**: Sejda
- **Budget**: PDF Candy
- **Desktop + Online**: SodaPDF
